Welcome to 000!

This guide will help you understand what's involved in taking ownership of your website and publishing it under your own accounts.

## What you'll need

- A GitHub account
- A Cloudflare account
- Access to your website's public repository
- A domain name (optional to get started)

## How it works

1. Explore your website and review the code.
2. Fork the repository to your GitHub account.
3. Connect your repository to Cloudflare.
4. Deploy your website.
5. Configure your domain and editing dashboard.
